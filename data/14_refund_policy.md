# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

### Monthly Subscriptions
- No refund for partial months
- Cancel anytime; access continues until end of billing period
- Exception: Service outage exceeding SLA guarantees

### Annual Subscriptions
- Full refund within 14 days of purchase (no questions asked)
- Prorated refund within 30 days of charge
- After 30 days: No refund, but subscription continues until expiration
- Exception: Material change in service terms

### Enterprise Contracts
- Governed by individual contract terms
- Standard: 30-day notice for termination
- Refund terms specified in the master service agreement

## How to Request a Refund
1. Go to Settings then Billing then Request Refund
2. Select the charge in question
3. Provide reason for refund request
4. Submit the request

Processing time: 5-10 business days
Refund method: Original payment method

## Non-Refundable Items
- Add-on purchases (storage, API calls) after usage
- Professional services (consulting, setup assistance)
- Third-party integration fees
- Domain registration fees

## Disputes
If you disagree with a billing decision:
1. Contact billing@cloudflow.io with your case
2. Include your account ID and charge details
3. Review within 5 business days
4. Escalation to management if unresolved

## Chargebacks
- Contact us before filing a chargeback
- Chargebacks may result in account suspension
- Resolution typically takes 15-30 days through the bank
