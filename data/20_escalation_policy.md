# Support Escalation Policy

## Support Tiers

### Tier 1: Self-Service
- Knowledge base articles
- Community forums
- In-app guided tours
- Video tutorials
- Available to: All plans

### Tier 2: Standard Support
- Email support
- Response time: 24 hours (Pro), 8 hours (Enterprise)
- Available: Monday-Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM (customer timezone)
- Scope: Product questions, basic troubleshooting, account issues

### Tier 3: Priority Support
- Dedicated support queue
- Response time: 4 hours (Enterprise only)
- Available: Monday-Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM
- Scope: Complex technical issues, integration problems, performance optimization

### Tier 4: Dedicated Support
- Named customer success manager
- Response time: 1 hour (Enterprise, custom agreements)
- Available: 24/7 for critical issues
- Scope: Architecture review, custom development, strategic planning

## Escalation Triggers
Automatic escalation occurs when:
- Issue unresolved after 3 interactions
- Customer explicitly requests escalation
- Billing dispute exceeding $500
- Security incident reported
- Data loss reported
- Service disruption affecting more than 10 users
- API outage affecting production systems

## Escalation Process
1. Support agent documents the issue and attempted solutions
2. Issue flagged with appropriate priority (P1-P4)
3. Routed to appropriate team
4. Customer notified of escalation and expected response time
5. Escalated agent contacts customer directly

## Priority Levels
- P1 (Critical): Service down, data loss, security breach - 1 hour response
- P2 (High): Major feature broken, significant impact - 4 hours response
- P3 (Medium): Feature partially working, workaround exists - 24 hours response
- P4 (Low): Minor issue, feature request, general question - 48 hours response

## Customer Communication
- Updates every 4 hours for P1/P2 issues
- Daily updates for P3/P4 issues
- Resolution summary provided upon closure
- Follow-up survey within 48 hours
