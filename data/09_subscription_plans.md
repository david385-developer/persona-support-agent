# Subscription Plans

## Plan Comparison

### Free Plan - $0
- Up to 5 users
- 3 projects
- 1 GB storage
- 10,000 API calls per day
- No SSO
- 2 webhook endpoints
- 5 custom fields
- 7-day audit logs
- No SLA guarantee
- Community support only
- CSV data export
- 3 automation workflows

### Pro Plan - $29 per user per month
- Up to 50 users
- Unlimited projects
- 50 GB storage
- 100,000 API calls per day
- No SSO
- 10 webhook endpoints
- 25 custom fields
- 90-day audit logs
- 99.9% SLA
- Email support with 24-hour response
- CSV, PDF, API export
- 50 automation workflows

### Enterprise Plan - Custom pricing
- Unlimited users
- Unlimited projects
- Unlimited storage
- 1,000,000 API calls per day
- SSO/SAML support
- Unlimited webhook endpoints
- Unlimited custom fields
- 1-year audit logs
- 99.99% SLA
- Dedicated support with 1-hour response
- All export formats plus custom
- Unlimited automation workflows

## How to Subscribe
1. Go to Settings then Billing
2. Select your plan
3. Enter payment details
4. Confirm subscription

## Cancellation
- Cancel anytime from Settings then Billing
- Free plan: No charges to reverse
- Pro plan: Active until end of billing period
- Enterprise plan: Contact your account manager

## Free Trial
Pro plan includes a 14-day free trial with full feature access.
No credit card required to start.
